# Defence-prime sample onboarding

Date: 2026-10-03
POC: Bothy team lead
TL;DR: Start at `/defense?mode=onboarding` to try a synthetic gallium investigation with four role handoffs and six stress checks. The guide explains expectations; it does not create cases, track completion, or validate a buyer workflow automatically.

## Positioning

Bothy helps a defence-prime supply-chain team establish what might be exposed to a disruption, inspect the supporting dependencies, and give verification a responsible owner. The output is a retained, cited case with review and finding reassessment. It is not a production-loss forecast, verified real-platform BOM, or an operational closure system.

## Exercise

Hypothesis: a gallium supply delay has been reported. This is an invented exercise condition, not a live alert. Use the starter's synthetic platform/part identities. Do not transfer a customer's BOM, sensitive supplier relationships, programme details, or credentials into the sample.

The guide keeps its instructions open while launching the actual investigation in a new tab. Analysis requires the graph service. Saved cases and role handoffs require configured SSO; the disposable local setup in [demo-sso-rehearsal.md](demo-sso-rehearsal.md) provides fixed synthetic accounts.

1. Analyst: analyze the gallium chain, reveal a captured sample, distinguish modeled connectivity from real procurement evidence, and draft a cited brief after sign-in.
2. Reviewer: inspect the stored citation, approve or reject verification with rationale, then assign an eligible owner and due time if approved.
3. Owner: sign in separately, reopen the same case, acknowledge and record a clearly synthetic finding. Do not invent a real inventory result.
4. Reviewer: reassess the returned finding with rationale. Acceptance is not operational closure. Further verification preserves the previous finding and requires separate follow-up via a linked revision; no task is assigned automatically.

The previous local SSO rehearsal demonstrates browser sign-in and ownership protocol integration. The latest reassessment and workspace changes still need the full live rehearsal on the final revision. Changing guide steps is not proof a backend transition succeeded.

## Stress checks

Use disposable records to test explanation clarity, exact citation inspection, denied wrong-role actions, failure with retained prior capture, conflicting duplicate decisions, and pending linked revisions preserving the parent finding. Record observed results and unexpected behavior separately. These checklist items are requests to test, not pre-certified outcomes.

## Discussion before private pilot

Ask who owns the disruption question, who may authorize verification, what evidence/reference set would support the decision, and what makes a finding acceptable. Measure current process time and interpretation errors before agreeing improvement targets. Confirm private access, hosting, retention, export controls, identity and transfer arrangements before introducing buyer data.
