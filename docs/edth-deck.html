Accountable by construction

Every step has a role. Every attempt is recorded.

  • Reviewed reads only. An exact query allowlist; arbitrary Cypher and writes are refused before reaching the graph. Fresh isolated client per read; HEAD resolves to a concrete pinned revision with query hash.
  • Deterministic cited briefs. Each claim links to its stored evidence row; the brief is bound to the hash of its evidence.
  • Real roles, fail-closed. Analyst drafts; reviewer approves and assigns; named owner acknowledges and reports. OIDC-verified — no role, no decision.
  • Hash-chained audit. Each entry commits to the one before it; a rewrite surfaces at the exact entry.

The interactive reviewer desk lets a jury try to cheat it: self-approve, replay the approval, acknowledge as the wrong person, edit the evidence, rewrite the audit log — each attempt is refused or detected against the real decision rules on a throwaway store.
